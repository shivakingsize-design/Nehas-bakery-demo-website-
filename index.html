About the bakery

A small bakery in Pune, with every cake made to order.

Neha's Bakery is run by Neha Bhalotia in Pune. Each order starts with a conversation about the occasion, the number of guests, the flavours you love and the look you have in mind. From there, each cake is planned and baked for that one celebration.

Ask about eggless and other dietary options when you enquire. Choices for each order are confirmed directly with the bakery.

Made with care, for your moments.
